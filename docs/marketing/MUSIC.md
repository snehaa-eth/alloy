Research date: 2026-11-19. The video is the 26s X teaser: "EVERYONE SAYS BUY AI. / WHICH STOCKS?", then the ticker wall collapses into one basket at 2s, then "pick a story / start with $5 / we trim drift / did followers win too / exit anytime", ending on INVEST IN STORIES, NOT SYMBOLS.

Ruled out by the founder: Kevin MacLeod / Incompetech (dated, sci-fi, cheesy), harsh industrial music (Cephalopod), and synth beeps.

**How sure is this?** Each claim below is tagged:

- **[U]** Unverified. It comes from my knowledge of the videos or from a weak third-party source. Watch the video before relying on it.

## 1. What high-end brands do

### Overall pattern
High-end product videos follow four rules:
1. **One music idea per video.** The track has a clear pulse or bass motif. There is no busy melody.
2. **Sound design does the storytelling.** Whooshes, clicks and hits are synced to frames. The music sits under them.
3. **Silence before the big moment.** The brand cuts the music for 100–500 ms before the reveal, then brings in a hit.
4. **A hard, clean ending.** The video ends on a downbeat or with a short reverb tail on the logo. Slow fades are rare.

Web sources on specific brands' music are thin. Most brands don't publish music credits for social cuts. Searches for Linear, Stripe Sessions, Arc and Robinhood music credits returned nothing specific.

### Sound design patterns that work
- **UI clicks:** Use them only on meaningful actions: pick, buy, confirm. Keep them quiet and the same every time. Many small clicks get more annoying than one strong cue ([open-design.ai](https://open-design.ai/blog/sound-design-product-videos/)) [V].
- **Whooshes:** Use one small family of sounds, timed to the peak acceleration of the motion ([same source](https://open-design.ai/blog/sound-design-product-videos/)) [V]. More than about 3 different whoosh sounds in 26s starts to sound like a template.
- **Risers:** Keep them short (0.5–1.5s) and filtered or reversed. Long EDM risers sound cheap.
- **Hits:** Use a sub drop or low "thud" plus a soft transient. Premium brands avoid metallic or industrial impacts.
- **Silence:** Use a gap of 100–300 ms right before the main hit, so the hit has something to contrast with.
- **Timing:** Many SFX files have 100–200 ms of silence before the transient. Trim it, or the sound will feel late ([open-design.ai](https://open-design.ai/blog/sound-design-product-videos/)) [V].
- **Comparing mixes:** Compare versions at the same loudness, because the louder one always seems better ([same source](https://open-design.ai/blog/sound-design-product-videos/)) [V].

## 3. The Alloy sound: 3 directions

The brand reference is "Aave/Pendle/Morpho × Robinhood/Coinbase". The visuals are near-black, burgundy and champagne gold, with editorial Anton type. So the sound should be **dark, warm, expensive and calm-confident**, not hype, not "corporate inspiring ukulele", not EDM.

### Direction A: Minimal piano and soft pulse ("Stripe Sessions / Robinhood brand film")
- **Tempo:** 70–95 BPM (or half-time feel).
- **Instrumentation:** Felt or muted piano playing 2–4 repeated notes, a warm sub-bass pulse, a soft side-chained pad, and optionally a light tick on the off-beat. No drums for the first 2s.
- **Feel:** Thoughtful, trustworthy, editorial. Good for the "did followers win too" and "exit anytime" beats.
- **Reference examples:** Robinhood "We are all investors" [V]; Stripe Sessions openers [U]; Emmit Fenn "Meteorite" (Apple XR reveal) [V] for the downtempo-meets-piano feel.
- **Avoid:**
  - "Inspiring corporate piano" with major-key arpeggios and strings swelling.
  - Sad solo piano (it reads as a charity ad).
  - Tracks tagged horror, spooky or gothic.
- **Risk:** It can feel too soft for X autoplay. It needs a strong sub hit at 2s.

### Direction B: Modern minimal electronic / future garage (recommended)
- **Tempo:** 120–135 BPM (future garage is about 130–140 BPM with half-time feel; minimal house about 120–124 BPM).
- **Instrumentation:**
  - Shuffled, soft-swung hats and rimshots
  - A deep, round sub-bass
  - Chopped airy vocal textures (no lyrics)
  - Warm Rhodes or pads
  - A clear "drop" or downbeat you can place at 2s
- **Feel:** Linear / Vercel / Raycast / Robinhood Gold. Night-time, premium, urban, confident.
- **Reference examples:** Odesza "Loyal" and Emmit Fenn "Meteorite" from Apple's event [V]; Linear and Vercel Ship release videos [U]; Robinhood Gold launch cuts [U].
- **Avoid:**
  - Big future-bass supersaw drops (too festival).
  - Tracks tagged "TikTok / energetic".
  - Cheesy "tech corporate" with plucky marimba.
  - Anything with obvious vocal lyrics, which compete with the on-screen copy.

### Direction C: Percussive "UI sound design only", no music bed ("Teenage Engineering / Coinbase QR / Apple Start Up")
- **Tempo:** Set by the edit. Build a grid at about 100–120 BPM and place every sound on it so the video still feels musical.
- **Instrumentation:**
  - Ticker "flicker" ticks for the wall of symbols
  - A filtered reverse riser (0.8s) into a **sub drop plus soft thud** at 2s, when the wall collapses
  - Soft UI taps on "pick a story" and "$5"
  - One soft whoosh family for transitions
  - A low, warm "chord" or single piano note on the final tagline
- **Feel:** The most "designed" and Apple-like option. It's very distinctive, but it takes the most work.
- **References:** Apple "Start Up" [V]; Coinbase QR [V]; Teenage Engineering product films [U].
- **Avoid:**
  - Bleepy synth "computer" sounds and metallic impacts (rejected as Cephalopod-style).
  - A different whoosh on every cut.
  - Glassy "notification ding" success chimes.
- **Risk:** With no bed, quiet moments sound empty on phone speakers. Fix this with a very low room-tone or pad drone at -30 dB.

## 5. Recommendation

**Direction B (modern minimal electronic / future garage), with Direction C sound design layered on top.** It matches the Linear / Vercel / Robinhood-Gold premium-dark feel. It has enough pulse to stop the scroll on X, and it keeps the screen copy readable.

**Top 3:**
1. **Cascade Breathe (Future Garage)**, NverAvetyanMusic: https://pixabay.com/music/beats-cascade-breathe-future-garage-412839/. Most proven and not Content ID registered.
2. **Minimal Underscore Piano Pulse**, Musinova: https://pixabay.com/music/ambient-minimal-underscore-piano-pulse-518251/. Darkest and most editorial. Its drumless and drum-and-bass stems make the 2s drop easy.
3. **Refined Motion**, u_98673jp944: https://pixabay.com/music/upbeat-refined-motion-408224/. Tagged luxury, premium, finance and piano.

### Mix plan for the 26s X teaser

| Time | Picture | Sound |
|---|---|---|
| 0.0–1.7s | "EVERYONE SAYS BUY AI. / WHICH STOCKS?" over the ticker wall | Track intro only, or the drumless stem, low-passed (about 800 Hz) and quiet at about -24 LUFS short-term. Add fast, quiet ticker flicker ticks. Optionally a 1s reverse riser that ends at 1.85s. |
| 1.85–2.0s | Wall starts to collapse | **Silence or "suck-out"** (cut everything for about 150 ms). |
| **2.0s** | Wall collapses into the basket | **The hit:** a sub drop plus the track's downbeat or drop, on the exact frame (frame 48 at 24 fps, 60 at 30 fps). Snap the low-pass filter open. Cut the track so its own drop or bar-1 downbeat lands here. Don't fade in. |
| 2–22s | The product beats ("pick a story", "$5", "we trim drift", "followers", "exit") | Full track. Cut the visuals on bar lines (at 130 BPM, 1 bar is about 1.85s). One soft UI click each on "pick" and "$5". Use one whoosh family, at -6 to -10 dB under the music. **Duck the music 3–6 dB for about 300 ms** under each important SFX (sidechain or manual keyframes). |
| 22–26s | INVEST IN STORIES, NOT SYMBOLS + logo | End the music **on a downbeat**: cut on the bar and let the reverb or tail ring for 1.5–2s, or apply a short fade of about 0.5–0.8s. Optionally add a single low piano note or chord on the tagline. No fade-out across the whole last 4s. |

**Loudness:**
- Export at **-14 LUFS integrated, -1 dBTP true peak**. This is the common social-video target. X doesn't publish an official target, so treat it as a best-practice default [U] ([source](https://openclip.app/learn/audio-normalization)).
- Keep the 2s hit the loudest moment. A short-term peak around -10 to -11 LUFS is fine.
- Check it on phone speakers. Sub drops vanish on phones, so layer a soft 100–200 Hz thud or transient on top of the sub.

**X plays video muted by default.** The video must read perfectly with no sound, and the audio is a bonus for people who tap to unmute. Don't put any information in the audio only.

**Normalize** with ffmpeg:

```bash
ffmpeg -i teaser.mp4 -af loudnorm=I=-14:TP=-1:LRA=11 -c:v copy -c:a aac -b:a 192k teaser_x.mp4
```
