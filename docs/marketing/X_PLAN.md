- unlock · seamless · revolutionize · game-changer · journey · dive in / deep dive

- rhetorical-question openers like "Ever wondered…?"

- More than one em dash per post (use a period or a colon instead)
- More than one emoji per post

**Mechanics.**
- Upload video natively. **The link always goes in the first reply**, never in the main post.
- Burn captions into every video.
- Max **2 posts from @alloy_fi per day** (author-diversity decay). The founder account can quote each one.
- Main slot **14:30 UTC** (NY 9:30am · Mumbai 8pm · Lagos 3:30pm · São Paulo 11:30am). Second slot **03:30 UTC** (Mumbai 9am · Manila 11:30am).
- **Reply to every reply for the first 60–90 min** after a post. Under the 2023 code, a reply the author answers weighed 75× a like.
- Compliance footer goes in the first reply on any post that shows the product:

**Quest rules that bite.**
- **Never** buy engagement, run alt accounts, or pay for testnet activity. The rules disqualify "manipulating engagement or traction metrics".
- Don't use the Base logo or the Coinbase logo.

## 2. Waitlist / traction hook

The scoring reportedly gives **50% to real onchain usage** *(unverified, from CryptoBriefing)*. So the hook should push people to **use the testnet**, not just leave an email.

1. **"Founding" numbered spots**, Robinhood-queue style but tied to real use.
   - The first **50 creators** who make a basket on testnet get a **Founding Creator #01–50** badge on their profile.
   - The first **500 people** who buy any basket get a **Founding Follower #** badge.
   - The number is public, so people screenshot it.
   - The badge is cosmetic only. **No token, no airdrop promise, no cash.** Paying for activity risks the "manipulated traction" rule.
2. **"Reply with a story, we'll build it."** On the teaser, ask for basket ideas. Build the best 3 live on stream or video and credit the person in the thesis.
   - Their basket = their post = their audience.
3. **Share card.** After someone makes a basket, the app makes a 1:1 card (the creator launch moment in MOTION.md) with the basket name, recipe and "built on Alloy". One tap shares it to X.
4. **Email capture** stays on the landing page for people who don't do testnet. Ask only: email, country, "what story would you buy?"
