## 1. What Alloy is (in one breath)
Alloy lets you invest in an *idea* instead of picking stocks. Example: "The AI Build-Out" is one basket of 5 US stocks.
- You start with **$5**.
- Creators build the baskets.
- Alloy keeps each basket balanced for you.
- You can see whether the people who followed a basket made money, not just its creator.
- You can sell anytime.

Tagline: **"Invest in stories, not symbols."**

Audience: everyday investors in India, Nigeria, Brazil and Indonesia, many of them new to investing and reading English as a second language. The film is watched on X, on phones, **mostly with the sound off**.

## 3. Look and feel
**Quality bar (technique only, don't copy the look):** this Base Batches promo by Jesse Pollak, https://x.com/jessepollak/status/2123176768915886137 (frames: `reference/base-batches-frames.png`). What we love about it:
- Real 3D objects rendered as fine lines in one colour.
- A persistent frame motif.
- Short, big text that decodes into place.
- Metallic/glassy transitions.
- Sound locked to the cuts.

**Alloy's own visual language** ("Certificate + Alloy bars"; see `style-frames/`):
- **Engraving.** Think share certificates and banknote intaglio. Objects (a microchip, a phone, coins, a rising chart) are rendered as **fine engraved hatching lines** in a jewel tone, with a subtle metallic sheen.
- **Certificate frame motif.** A hairline border, a mono serial like `No. 0001 · ALLOY` in a corner, and a thin burgundy rule. This replaces brackets; please don't use brackets.
- **Alloy bars.** A basket's recipe shown as stacked bands, one per stock, thickness = weight, in metallic jewel tones. Use them for transitions too, like molten metal bars.
- **UI shots** must match `style-frames/cards-v4.png` and `wireframes-v4-*.png`: sharp 4 px corners, hairline frames, a guilloche header, a big figure, square-edged buttons.
- **Basket cover art** for backgrounds and transitions: `brand/covers/`.

- **Type:**
  - **Anton** (Google Fonts): one hero line per scene, for the wordmark and big figures.
  - **Inter** 500/600: all script lines, about 88 px at 1080×1920.
  - **JetBrains Mono**: serials and tickers.
- **Logo:** the type-only "ALLOY" wordmark in Anton. There is no symbol yet.
- **Don'ts:**
  - No mascot, no statues, no cartoon people.
  - No neon purple or "crypto bro" glow.
  - No return numbers or charts with values.
  - Never say or show "backed by Coinbase/Base".
  - No stock-photo people.
  - Don't let the text move while it's being read.

## 5. Sound
- Music: one of four Pixabay tracks we've shortlisted (free commercial licence): Refined Motion, Elegant Minimal, Minimal Electronica, Chill Abstract (Intention). The files are available on request. You're welcome to propose a properly licensed alternative.
- Restrained sound design: soft UI clicks, a short silence before the fold (scene 3), and a subtle hit on the wordmark.
- Mix about −16 to −18 LUFS. The film must work with the sound off.
