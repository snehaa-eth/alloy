# Alloy explainer: 3 script concepts (writing only, no visuals yet)

Status: draft for founder approval, 2026-11-19. Research and rules: `RESEARCH.md`. Comprehension test: `USER_TEST.md`.

## Rules every script follows

1. **Sound off first.** The on-screen text tells the whole story. Voiceover (VO) is optional and never adds a new fact.
2. **Hold rule** (RESEARCH §3.3): hold = 1.0 s + words ÷ 2.5, rounded up to 0.5 s, minimum 2.0 s.
   - At most 8 words per card. Only the end card, which carries the legal line, goes over.
   - "Req." in the tables = the rule's minimum. "Hold" = what we use. Hold is never below Req.
3. **One new idea per scene.** About 6–7 ideas per video (RESEARCH §2).
4. **Text placement.** Text sits in the same place every time (upper third). Text appears whole, never word by word. Motion happens below the text or between cards.
5. **Plain words.**
   - "Basket" is defined on screen the first time ("5 US stocks, one idea").
   - Never on screen: NAV, rebalance, drift, token(ized), vault, USDC, recipe, thesis. The fixed legal line is the only exception.
6. **VO pace.** VO stays under 150 wpm. Every VO line fits inside its scene at 2.5 words/s.
7. **Fixed end card**, shared by all three concepts:
   - `Follow @alloy_fi for early access.` (5 words)
   - `Tokenized stock exposure, not shares. Not available to US persons. You can lose money.` (14 words)
   - 19 words → Req. 8.6 s → **hold 9.0 s**.
   - Swap the CTA line for a demo link when one is live. Keep it at ≤6 words.
8. **Example chart.** Any follower chart is labelled `Example basket. Past results aren't a promise.` (7 words). It shows no % numbers and no y-axis values. Its words count toward that card's hold.

## Recommendation: Concept C (story first), short cut first

Why C:
1. **It fixes the failure exactly.**
   - The user said "the narrative isn't understandable." C is the only concept that is a *narrative*: one person, cause, then effect.
   - A and the old teaser are lists of features. A list is hard to follow when you miss one item. A story isn't, because each step explains the next.
2. **It's the easiest to retell.** The recall test (USER_TEST.md) asks people to explain Alloy back. "Priya didn't know which stocks, so she bought the idea for $5" is a sentence people can repeat to a friend. That's Asha's stated goal in UX_MAP.
3. **It keeps A's strong hook.** Card C2 is A's "which stocks?" tension, so we don't give up the hook.
4. **It shows the differentiators as actions, not claims.** "She checks: did followers make money too?" and "She does nothing" show the follower results and auto-trim through what Priya does.

How to proceed:
1. Approve wording.
2. Make a **text-only animatic** of C short (50 s): white text on black at these holds.
3. Run USER_TEST.md with 2–3 people.
4. Only then do visuals.

Keep **A** as the fallback if a person on screen is too costly to make well. Save **B** (the playlist) for video #2, once people know what Alloy is.
