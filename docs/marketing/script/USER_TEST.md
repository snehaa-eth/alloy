# Script comprehension test (5 questions)

Use this on 2–3 people before any visuals are made. Ideal testers:
- one first-time investor (like Asha: has never bought a US stock),
- one person reading English as a second language,
- one crypto-native person.

None of them should have heard of Alloy.

## How to run it (10 minutes per person)
1. **Show it once.** Use the text-only animatic (white text on black at the script's hold times), or read the on-screen lines aloud at a normal pace. Don't explain, pause or replay.
2. **Ask the 5 questions in order.** Write down their words exactly. Don't correct them or say "close".
3. Ask question 1 **before** any others. The later questions give away answers.

## The 5 questions
1. **"In one sentence, what does Alloy do?"**
   - Pass: they mention all three of
     - (a) a group of stocks,
     - (b) built around one idea or theme,
     - (c) you can start small.
   - Any word for "group" counts. They don't need to say "basket".
2. **"How much money do you need to start?"**
   - Pass: "$5".
3. **"What happens if one stock in the basket grows much bigger than the others?"**
   - Pass: "Alloy trims it back" or "they balance it for you" and that **they don't have to do anything.**
4. **"Before you put money in, how could you tell if a basket has been good for the people who joined it?"**
   - Pass: "It shows how followers did, not just the creator."
   - Fail signals: "the creator's returns" or "I couldn't".
5. **"Was anything confusing, too fast, or did anything make you not trust it?"**
   - Note every word they stumble on and every "wait, what…".
   - Also listen for whether they understood "Not available to US persons" and "You can lose money".

## Score it
| Result | What to do |
|---|---|
| Q1 correct for **≥2 of 3** people, and Q2–Q4 mostly correct | Script approved. Start on visuals. |
| Q1 fails for 2+ people | Message problem. Rewrite the first 15 s (hook + basket definition) before anything else. |
| Q1 passes but one of Q2–Q4 fails for 2+ people | That scene's line is unclear. Rewrite that card only, and recheck its hold time. |
| Anyone says "too fast" or asks to replay | Add 0.5 s to every card, or cut one idea. Don't shrink the text. |
| Someone thinks they "own shares" or that returns are promised | Compliance risk. Fix the wording before anything else, even if comprehension passed. |

## Quick extra check (optional, 30 seconds)
Ask them to **tell a friend about it out loud, as if they were sending a voice note**.

The phrases they use are your best copy. Paste them into SCRIPTS.md beside the line they replace.
