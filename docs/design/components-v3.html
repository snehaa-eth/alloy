Type3 sizes per screen: 32 hero · 15 body · 12 label/meta. Inter 400/600, JetBrains Mono 400/500 for labels, meta and numbers in rows. Anton only for ONE hero moment (Founding #, landing headline).
Spacing4-pt scale: 4 · 8 · 12 · 16 · 20 · 24 · 32. Screen gutter 16; card padding 16; gaps 8/12.
Radii12 inputs + banners · 20 cards + sheets · pill (999) buttons, chips, segmented · 50% avatars, logos
Touch≥ 44px for every target (buttons 48, quiet links 44, nav items 44)
IconsLucide (ISC licence): one 24-grid set, 1.75 stroke, rounded caps; already a dependency of apps/web (lucide-react, tree-shaken per icon). Phosphor was the alternative, but mixing weights invites inconsistency.
ColourTokens only (TOKENS.md v0.3). Burgundy ≤ 10%, gold ≤ 3% per screen. Colour comes from content (jewel tones, logos).
