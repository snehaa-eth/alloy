How to read hexes: a plain hex was confirmed by at least two sources, or by a brand page. "approx." means it came from one aggregator or was estimated from the logo or app. Brand pages were not opened, so check against the official kit before final use.

## 2. Crowded vs open

- **Crowded:** blue (the default for "trust"), green (also the gain colour), purple (the neobank and DeFi favourite: Nubank, PhonePe, Kraken, Phantom), yellow/gold (owned by Binance in crypto), black (Revolut, OKX).
- **Some use:** pink/coral (Uniswap owns hot pink).
- **Almost empty:** burgundy/oxblood (one legacy Indian bank), copper/bronze (none), deep petrol teal (none), olive/moss (none as a lead colour).

## 3. Current palette: keep, adjust or replace?

Current: bg #0A0A0A, text #F2F0ED, brand burgundy #6D001A / #8C1230, accent crimson #C41A42, gold #C9A96E, Buy button cream #F2F0ED, gain #3DBE8B, loss #FF6A3D.

**Verdict: keep the burgundy and change the details around it.**

- **Is burgundy taken?** Almost not. No fintech app, exchange, wallet or DeFi protocol leads with it. The only clash is Axis Bank in India: its maroon logo and its premium tier, "Burgundy". That is a bank, not an investing app, so the risk is small. Do not name anything in the app "Burgundy".
- **Crimson #C41A42 is the problem.** On a money screen, bright crimson reads as "loss" or "error". Remove it as an accent.
- **The loss colour #FF6A3D is orange.** That keeps it away from the brand, but people read loss as red. Orange-for-loss looks unusual, and it will clash with any copper or gold. Use a clear coral-red such as #F0545C. It differs from the dark burgundy by brightness, so the two will not be confused.
- **Champagne gold #C9A96E is fine but common.** Gold signals "premium" for every private bank and for Binance. Keep it rare or replace it.
- **The cream Buy button works:** it is neutral, high-contrast and never confused with gain or loss. But it carries no brand.
- **Burgundy is very dark.** On a black background it shows up only as a logo dot and a thin line (see the swatch board). It works as identity but cannot carry action.
- **Culture:** maroon and red are auspicious in India (weddings, festivals) and lucky in East Asia. In parts of West Africa (Ghana, Nigeria), bright red with black can mean mourning or danger. Deep burgundy softens this.

## 4. Five directions

All directions use the same gain green #3DBE8B. Loss is a coral-red, #F0545C, unless noted.

### 1. Oxblood & Champagne (light adjustment)
- **Hexes:** brand #6D0F24 · champagne #C9A96E · ink #0B0909
- **Buy button:** cream #F2F0ED with dark text (as now)
- **Feel:** old money, private-bank calm
- **Culture:** gold means wealth everywhere in these regions, especially India. It can look "luxury, not for me" to a first-time $5 investor.
- **Gain/loss clash:** low, once crimson is gone
- **Nearest brand:** Axis Bank burgundy; Binance gold for the accent

### 2. Oxblood & Copper (recommended)
- **Hexes:** brand #6D0F24 · copper #C77B4A · ink #0C0A0A
- **Buy button:** copper #C77B4A with dark text (contrast about 4.8:1)
- **Feel:** warm and grown-up. Two metals together tell the "Alloy" story.
- **Culture:** copper is familiar and positive: Indian copper vessels, Zambia and Chile copper economies, terracotta and bronze craft across LATAM and SE Asia. It has no political or religious meaning. (Saffron does in India, so stay away from it.)
- **Gain/loss clash:** low with loss #F0545C. Do not keep orange loss #FF6A3D, because it sits too close to copper.
- **Nearest brand:** MetaMask orange (#F6851B, much brighter) and Bybit (#F7A600). No finance app uses copper.

### 3. Copper Alloy (fresh)
- **Hexes:** brand #B8673A · bronze #2A1D17 · pale brass #E9D8C4
- **Buy button:** copper #C77B4A with dark text
- **Feel:** warm, earthy, handmade. The most ownable option.
- **Culture:** same as copper above, and warmer overall
- **Gain/loss clash:** medium. The whole screen is warm, so loss must be a pink-red (#F0476B) to stand apart.
- **Nearest brand:** MetaMask (#F6851B)
- **Cost:** gives up the burgundy you already have.

### 4. Petrol & Brass (fresh)
- **Hexes:** brand #0F4C55 · brass #C6A15B · ink #071416
- **Buy button:** brass #C6A15B with dark text
- **Feel:** calm, deep and trustworthy without being blue
- **Culture:** teal is neutral and positive in all four regions. Green-blue tones read as "Islamic-friendly" in Indonesia, Malaysia and northern Nigeria.
- **Gain/loss clash:** medium. Teal is close to green, so make gain brighter (#4ADE80) and never use the brand colour on numbers.
- **Nearest brand:** Pendle and Hyperliquid (bright aqua/mint), Groww (#00D09C). All are far lighter.

### 5. Olive & Bone (fresh)
- **Hexes:** brand #5B6332 · bone #ECE6D6 · ink #11120D
- **Buy button:** bone #ECE6D6 with dark text
- **Feel:** quiet, grounded, "not a casino"
- **Culture:** in parts of LATAM and Africa, olive can read as military uniform. It is also the least energetic option.
- **Gain/loss clash:** medium. Olive is a green, so keep it off numbers.
- **Nearest brand:** Wise forest green (approx. #163300, used only as a secondary colour)

## 5. Pick: Oxblood & Copper

- **Keeps what you have.** Burgundy is already the brand, and burgundy is almost unclaimed in fintech and DeFi.
- **Fixes the weak spots.** Crimson goes because it looked like "loss". Common gold becomes copper, which no finance app uses. Loss becomes a true coral-red, so people read it at a glance.
- **Gives the Buy button a brand colour.** The cream button was safe but generic. The copper button is safe and recognisably Alloy.
- **Tells the name.** Two metals mixed: oxblood plus copper is literally an alloy.

Proposed tokens: brand #6D0F24, action/Buy #C77B4A (pressed #A9633A), ink #0C0A0A, card #1B1213, text #F2EDE8, gain #3DBE8B, loss #F0545C. Use the champagne gold #C9A96E only if a "rare highlight" is still needed, and sparingly.
