Landing notes

  1. Headline option 1 is shown (it's already the X bio). Alternatives: "BUY THE WHOLE IDEA." / "ONE TAP. A WHOLE THEME." Anton at 120px carries the moodboard on its own, so there's no need for gradient tricks.
  2. Two CTAs only. Explore is the primary (off-white fill on dark; burgundy would be 1.57:1); Build is the outline. Visitors can browse without signing up (Robinhood/feel.cash pattern) and only sign up at Buy.
  3. One art slot, art-directed (bust, headset, silk, smoke). It's the only "decorative" asset in the product. Keep it off product screens.
  4. Live cards show follower return first and creator return second, so the main differentiator appears above the fold.
  5. Legal is written as plain sentences, not a disclaimer block. No "backed by" or "partnered with" anywhere (ARCH §7).

Explore notes

  1. The default sort is follower return, not creator return or APR. This avoids Hyperliquid-style "lucky week" leaderboards. The alternative default is "Most followed" (OPEN_QUESTIONS #3).
  2. The follower number is bigger and coloured; the creator number is neutral and smaller. That's the hierarchy of the whole product.

  4. Story baskets carry the disclosure date and "Not affiliated" on the card itself, not hidden on the detail page.
  5. Pending recipe changes show on the card (burgundy pill), so nobody buys blind into a change.

Buy notes

  1. The breakdown is collapsed by default. It auto-expands if price impact is over 0.5% or the market is closed.
  2. The fee is spelled out next to the buy button, not in an FAQ. This answers the complaint Dub users raise.

  5. Success sets expectations for what happens next instead of celebrating. No confetti.
