The Alloy register

Tokenized stocks are digital share certificates, so Alloy's documents look like certificates: an engraved guilloche drawn from each basket's real prices, a burgundy signature rule, a serial, the figure in Anton, and the recipe as an alloy bar. Ten instruments cover every moment, from holding to amending.
