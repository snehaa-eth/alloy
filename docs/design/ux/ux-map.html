1 · Sitemap and nav model

Four tabs: Explore, Portfolio, Activity, Me. Studio appears only after you create a basket. Sheets (dashed) open over the current screen and return you to it. Logged-out visitors land on Landing or a shared basket page; judges enter at /try.

7 · Recipe-change notice and the 24h window (follower)

The one time Alloy interrupts holders. Doing nothing means staying in. Exits are equal-weight and one tap.

flowchart TD
  P(["Creator proposes: RecipeProposed (24h, or 10 min on demo baskets)"]) --> N["Notify holders + watchers: in-app Needs you, email, push/Telegram if on"]
  N --> V["Change sheet: before/after, quoted reason, what moves for your $, est. cost, time in local zone"]
  V --> CH{"Your choice"}
  CH -- "Keep (or do nothing)" --> K["Stay in; reminders stop if chosen explicitly"]
  CH -- "Sell" --> SL["Exit sheet, Get USDC, 100% prefilled"]
  CH -- "Get the stocks" --> WD["Exit sheet, Get the stocks, 100% prefilled"]
  K --> RM{"Chosen explicitly?"}

  WAIT -- "time reached, market open, feeds healthy" --> EXE["Keeper executes; Rebalanced"]
  WAIT -- "time reached, market closed or feed paused/stale" --> WT["Due, waiting for market or healthy prices; exits stay open"]:::cond --> EXE

8 · Drift trim and market / feed conditions

Trims are executed only by Alloy's keeper, never need user action, and are explained after the fact. Conditions below decide what still works.

flowchart TD
  K0(["Keeper loop every 60s"]) --> MD{"Basket mode"}
  MD -- "Balanced" --> B1{"Any stock outside band (5 pts / 25%)?"}
  MD -- "Let Winners Run" --> B2{"Any stock over 40% cap?"}
  MD -- "Trend-aware" --> B3{"Outside band AND 2 closes below 50-day MA?"}
  B1 -- no --> NONE["Nothing to do"]
  B2 -- no --> NONE
  B3 -- no --> NONE
  B1 -- yes --> G
  B2 -- yes --> G
