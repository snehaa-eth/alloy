0 · Principles: say less, colour with purpose

One idea per screen. Numbers and visuals over sentences. Explanations behind a tap. Max one helper line (≤ 8 words) per block; labels 1–3 words. Numbers in the viewer's locale (en-IN lakh grouping, pt-BR, id-ID); USD stays the currency.

3 · Sign-up, in-app browsers and attribution

Triggered only by a money action. Email code by default. Attribution (s, p, ref, utm) is stored before sign-in and attached to sign-in, buy and create.

5 · Buy

One sheet is the review; tap to confirm. "≈ you get" comes from the live DEX quote, not the oracle Value. Buy works 24/7 (no fresh feed needed). Presets $10 · $25 · $100, no Max. No TVL cap on testnet.

flowchart TD
  B0(["Buy on basket page"]) --> AU{"Signed in + attested?"}
  AU -- no --> SU["Sign-up + auto test dollars"] --> B1
  AU -- yes --> B1["Buy sheet: amount, presets 10 / 25 / 100, logo row with $"]
  B1 --> AM{"Amount ≥ $5 and ≤ balance?"}
  AM -- "below $5" --> A1["Minimum $5"]:::edge --> B1
  AM -- "short" --> A2["Add test money inline"] --> B1
  AM -- ok --> Q["Live quote: ≈ you get · Fee 1%/yr · Cost ≈ $0.03 (pool fees + impact)"]
  Q --> GAP{"Fill vs Value gap over 0.5%?"}
  GAP -- yes --> G1["Amber chip: fills ~0.8% lower now · Why? (market closed, pools move)"]:::cond --> PC
  GAP -- no --> PC{"Basket state"}
  PC -- "pending change" --> P1["Chip: changes in 14h → Change sheet"]:::cond --> SIM
  PC -- "global pause" --> P2["New buys paused; sell and Get the stocks work"]:::edge
  PC -- ok --> SIM{"Simulate"}
  SIM -- "PolicyForbids" --> S1["One stock can't be bought now; nothing sent"]:::edge
  SIM -- ok --> CF["Buy $25 (tap, fixed 1% slippage)"]
  CF --> TX{"Transaction"}
  TX -- success --> OK(["Founding Follower #N · Your slice: 5 stocks · Refunded $0.42 if any · SHARE (primary)"])
  TX -- "slippage revert" --> X1["Nothing spent; try again"]:::edge
  TX -- "pending / unknown" --> X2["Poll by tx hash; leave allowed; no second submit"]:::edge
  TX -- "generic fail" --> X4["Your USDC is still there"]:::edge

Leftovers: tiny dust is donated to the basket; larger leftovers are refunded as USDC. No stray stock dust in holdings. Mainnet proof only: $5k cap → "Full" / clamp on Value.

6 · Exit: Get USDC, Get the stocks, claim

One Exit sheet, two equal options, 24/7 including weekends. Get the stocks can never be paused. A blocked token transfer becomes "held for you"; claim is one tap per token, signed by you (caller-only), gas covered.

  E1 --> MODE{"How?"}
  MODE -- "Get USDC (default)" --> S1["≈ $37.92 from quote · you keep $88.48 · Cost ≈ $0.05"]
  S1 --> SC{"Checks"}

  SC -- "impact over 1%" --> SI["Warn: smaller or Get the stocks"]:::edge --> SCF

7 · Recipe-change notice and window (follower)

Doing nothing means staying in. The countdown is the exact executableAt; a drift trim never delays it. A replaced proposal is a new notice with a diff, and everyone's choice resets.

flowchart TD
  P(["RecipeProposed: 24h, or 10 min on demo baskets"]) --> N["Email + Activity Needs you + status strip"]
  N --> V["Change sheet: before→after bars · reason · ≈ $14 of yours moves · countdown to executableAt"]
  V --> CH{"Your choice"}
  CH -- "Keep (or nothing)" --> K["You're staying"]
  CH -- "Sell" --> SL["Exit sheet, Get USDC, 100%"]
  CH -- "Get the stocks" --> WD["Exit sheet, Get the stocks, 100%"]
  K --> WAIT{"Before executableAt"}
  WAIT -- "creator replaces" --> RP["New email + diff vs previous · timer restarts · choices reset"]:::edge --> V
  WAIT -- "creator cancels" --> CN["Cancelled with reason; nothing changes"]:::edge

  MK -- no --> WT["Due · runs at market open / when prices are healthy; exits stay open"]:::cond --> MK
  MK -- yes --> EXE["Keeper executes (trims never block it)"]
  EXE -- "large change" --> MD["Changing · day 1 of 2"]:::cond --> DN

8 · Drift trims and the market matrix

Only keeper rebalances need fresh feeds. Buy, sell and Get the stocks work 24/7. Trims are explained in Activity only (no email) and never delay a due recipe change.

flowchart TD
  K0(["Keeper loop every 60s"]) --> MD{"Basket mode"}
  MD -- "Balanced" --> B1{"Stock outside band?"}
  MD -- "Let Winners Run" --> B2{"Stock over 40% cap?"}
  MD -- "Trend-aware" --> B3{"Outside band and trend broken?"}
  B1 -- no --> NONE["Nothing to do"]
  B2 -- no --> NONE
  B3 -- no --> NONE
  B1 -- yes --> G
  B2 -- yes --> G
  B3 -- yes --> G{"Guards: market open, feeds fresh and unpaused, loss caps"}
  G -- pass --> T["Trim (chunked if large)"]
  G -- fail --> DF["Deferred; Details: Rebalance waiting + reason (no notification)"]:::cond
  T --> FD["Activity: Trimmed NVDA 41→40% · value unchanged except $0.06"]
  T -- partial --> PT["Part way; more tomorrow"]:::edge
ConditionBuySellGet the stocksRebalanceUser sees
Market open, feeds healthyYesYesYesYesStrip hidden / "Open"
Market closed (nights, weekends, holidays)YesYesYesWaitsStrip "Closed · opens 8:00 PM" (local); amber gap chip only if fill differs from Value > 0.5%
Feed stale (>25h) or pausedYesYesYesBlockedDetails: rebalances paused; due change "waiting for healthy prices"
Global pauseNoYesYesNoStrip "New buys paused"

10 · Propose, replace, cancel (creator, in Me → Your baskets)

Creators only propose or cancel, always with a reason. Alloy's keeper executes. Editing a pending change restarts the 24h notice and resets followers' choices.
